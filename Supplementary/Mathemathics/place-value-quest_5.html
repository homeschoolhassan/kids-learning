Place Value & Reading Numbers

Explore how every digit has a place — and a value!

🏠 What is Place Value?

Every digit in a number has a place. Its place tells you what it is worth. The same digit can mean different things depending on where it sits!

Look at the number 345:
3
Hundreds
= 300
+
4
Tens
= 40
+
5
Ones
= 5
=
345

👥 Numbers Live in Groups of Three

Big numbers are split into groups of 3 digits, each group has its own name. Colour shows which group each digit belongs to!

📌 Commas Make Reading Easy

We put a comma between each group of three. This makes big numbers much easier to read.

Hard to read:
4582917
→
Easy to read:
4,582,917
📍 4 million  |  582 thousand  |  917 ones

🧩 Expanded Form

We can "expand" a number to show each digit's true value. It's like unwrapping a present!

🗣️ How to Read a Big Number

Read the digits in each group, say the group name, move to the next group. Don't say "ones" at the very end!

Step-by-step: 7,345,291
1️⃣  7 → "seven" → +million → seven million
2️⃣  345 → "three hundred forty-five" → +thousand → three hundred forty-five thousand
3️⃣  291 → "two hundred ninety-one" → (no "ones"!)
✅  "seven million three hundred forty-five thousand two hundred ninety-one"
