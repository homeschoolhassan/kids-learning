For grown-ups: how to use this book

  1. Read one wisdom together. One a night or a few a week works well.
  2. Ask the Talk About It question and listen more than you speak.
  3. Do the Try It activity during the week, then check in on how it went.

Written for reading aloud with children about 5 to 10. Older readers can read it alone. Each idea comes from real psychology, simplified for kids.
